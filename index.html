Why Iron Haven

A gym built around the work, not the mirror

Most clubs sell access to equipment. We coach the session. Every member gets a movement screen, a written plan and a coach who knows their name and their numbers.

  • Coached first 6 weeks. Every new member is programmed and supervised before training alone.
  • Capped at 12 per class. Small enough that a coach can actually watch your sets.
  • Club-standard platforms. Every bench, rack and platform is repaired or replaced within 30 days.
  • Progress tracked for you. Bench, squat and deadlift are logged and charted automatically.
